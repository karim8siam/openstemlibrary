❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know About OpenSTEM

Answers to common questions regarding our free university textbooks, interactive physics simulations, academic curriculum adoption, and mathematical derivations.

What is OpenSTEM Digital Academic Press? ▼
OpenSTEM Digital Academic Press is an open-access global university initiative providing 100% free, mathematically rigorous digital textbooks and interactive computer simulations across Physics, Mathematics, and Engineering sciences for university students and professors worldwide.
Are all textbooks, derivations, and simulations completely free? ▼
Yes, absolutely. All textbooks, full step-by-step mathematical proofs without skipped steps, 60 FPS numerical simulations, and problem sets are completely free to read, bookmark, and study worldwide with zero paywalls, mandatory accounts, or subscription fees.
What subjects and university courses are available? ▼
The library currently hosts 27 comprehensive university courses including Classical Mechanics, Quantum Mechanics I & II, Electrodynamics, Statistical Mechanics, Solid State Physics I & II, Nuclear Physics I & II, Wave & Modern Optics, Calculus I, II & III, Linear Algebra, 2D/3D Geometry, Digital Electronics, and Astrophysics & Cosmology.
How do the real-time interactive simulations work? ▼
Each textbook embeds hardware-accelerated HTML5 Canvas simulations calculated with numerical integration algorithms (such as Runge-Kutta 4th order and Verlet methods). Students can tweak physical parameters in real time to visualize abstract mathematical physics concepts dynamically.
Can university professors and instructors adopt OpenSTEM for courses? ▼
Yes. All OpenSTEM materials are open educational resources (OER) designed for undergraduate and graduate STEM curricula. Instructors are welcome to assign chapters, problem sets, and interactive simulation labs directly to their students without licensing fees or permissions.
How are mathematical accuracy and peer review maintained? ▼
All textbook content adheres strictly to gold-standard academic literature (citing Griffiths, Goldstein, Jackson, Spivak, Strang, and modern peer-reviewed journals). Mathematical derivations are presented with unskipped algebraic steps, and an active errata desk reviews user reports to ensure error-free mathematical rigor.
