How do the real-time interactive simulations work? ▼
Each textbook embeds hardware-accelerated HTML5 Canvas simulations calculated with numerical integration algorithms (such as Runge-Kutta 4th order and Verlet methods). Students can tweak physical parameters in real time to visualize abstract mathematical physics concepts dynamically.
Can university professors and instructors adopt OpenSTEM for courses? ▼
Yes. All OpenSTEM materials are open educational resources (OER) designed for undergraduate and graduate STEM curricula. Instructors are welcome to assign chapters, problem sets, and interactive simulation labs directly to their students without licensing fees or permissions.
How should I check accuracy and report an error? ▼
The library provides worked explanations and simulations for self-study. References and automated checks do not establish independent peer review or guarantee error-free content. Compare results with your course materials and report suspected errors with a page URL and supporting reasoning. See About & Editorial for review limits and contact details.
