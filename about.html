About OpenSTEM Press

Democratizing Higher STEM Education for the World

OpenSTEM is an open-access digital publishing initiative founded on a single conviction: rigorous, university-standard mathematical and scientific education should be universally accessible to every curious mind on Earth, 100% free of charge.

Our Three Pillars

📐

Mathematical Rigor

No hand-waving or oversimplified analogies. Every physical law is derived step-by-step from foundational first principles using complete vector calculus and operator algebra.

⚡

Inline Interactive Simulations

Theory paired directly with experimentation. Real-time HTML5 Canvas physics models allow students to see fields evolve, particles tunnel, and waves disperse.

🌍

Universal Global Access

Completely free open access funded through ethical, non-intrusive academic sponsorships and Google AdSense, eliminating textbook paywalls forever.

Academic Editorial Standards & Peer-Review Methodology

Every textbook, chapter, and derivation published across the OpenSTEM platform adheres to rigorous international higher education standards. To ensure absolute scientific and pedagogical integrity:

  • Curriculum Benchmarks: All physical derivations and mathematical proofs are cross-verified against canonical university literature, including works by Griffiths, Jackson, Sakurai, Goldstein, Halliday & Resnick, Apostol, Spivak, and Strang.
  • No Mathematical Gaps: Intermediate algebraic steps, tensor contractions, coordinate transformations, and contour integrals are derived in full without omitting steps.
  • Interactive Simulation Fidelity: All numerical simulation engines utilize 60 FPS Runge-Kutta, Euler, and matrix eigensystem algorithms, running natively in the client browser with verifiable numerical parameters.

Academic Errata & Peer Review Channel

Science is a collaborative global effort. We welcome peer reviews, typographical reports, and mathematical corrections from university faculty, researchers, and students worldwide.
