Lesson 5.1 — Plate bending stiffness and foundations

For a homogeneous isotropic thin slab, flexural rigidity grows with thickness cubed. The slab is supported by a finite foundation, so actual wheel-load stress requires plate-on-foundation analysis or calibrated design guidance.
