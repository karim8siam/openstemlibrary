§4.1 Foundational Principles & Theory: Hydrotreating, Desulfurization and Catalytic Reforming

[Book navigation](../index.html) · [Assessment](../assessments/unit-04.html) · [Applied project](../projects/unit-04.html)

Unit target

Quantify contaminant removal and identify the boundary between screening equations and actual treating-system design.

Engineering context

Sulfur compounds, acid gases, water and trace impurities affect corrosion, safety, emissions and catalyst life. Treating is often integrated with refinery fuel gas and sulfur recovery; the removal targets are typically product- and jurisdiction-specific. Simple exponential-removal equations are valuable for sensitivity but not guaranteed design performance. The modeler should decide what constitutes a material stream, what was measured directly, and which quantities follow from an equation. Whenever possible, start with an inlet-and-outlet balance in physical units rather than with an index or a normalized graph. An index can be useful for a classroom sensitivity exercise, but it can also conceal the absolute process magnitude needed to estimate utilities, storage or emissions.

Decision boundary

State inlet composition, measurement basis, removal target, allowable residual, sampling method and disposal or regeneration route before selecting equipment. A good recommendation distinguishes what is established by conservation from what depends on kinetic, thermodynamic or operating assumptions. Record your data source and test method. Identify unknown parameters before asserting any performance. If two alternatives seem very close numerically, compute the range of results that plausible uncertainty could produce rather than picking the one with more decimals.

Technical study sequence

  1. [Hydrodesulfurization and sulfur balances](../lessons/unit-04-lesson-01.html) — [interactive lab 10](../labs/lab-10.html)
  2. [Acid-gas removal and amine circulation](../lessons/unit-04-lesson-02.html) — [interactive lab 11](../labs/lab-11.html)
  3. [Gas dehydration and water dew-point control](../lessons/unit-04-lesson-03.html) — [interactive lab 12](../labs/lab-12.html)
  4. [Sulfur recovery and tail-gas treatment](../lessons/unit-04-lesson-04.html) — [interactive lab 12](../labs/lab-12.html)

Laboratory strategy

These three distinct models illuminate different physics or accounting relationships in the unit: Hydrodesulfurization residual sulfur, Amine contactor removal proxy, Gas dehydration approach. Take each model as a conditional hypothesis. For one model, reconstruct the default answer from the equation; for a second, find a limiting case; for the third, propose a measurement that could disprove it. A smooth trend is not an indicator of industrial accuracy, and sensitivity does not establish causality when the underlying variables are correlated.

Deliverable and independent scrutiny

Prepare a two-page engineering memo with a material and energy boundary, one calculation table, at least two uncertainty or failure scenarios, and a decision that names the data still needed. Use the [16 question assessment](../assessments/unit-04.html) to identify technical gaps. The [applied project](../projects/unit-04.html) then adds constraints from adjacent units so that a local process improvement can be evaluated for its plant-wide consequence.

§4.2 Engineering Topic 4.2: Unit 04 · Lesson 01 — Hydrodesulfurization and sulfur balances

Unit 04 · Lesson 01 — Hydrodesulfurization and sulfur balances

[Unit map](../units/unit-04.html) · [Open numerical laboratory](../labs/lab-10.html) · [Assessment](../assessments/unit-04.html) · [Applied project](../projects/unit-04.html)

The engineering problem

Hydrodesulfurization converts many organosulfur compounds toward hydrogen sulfide using hydrogen and catalyst. Different sulfur species show different kinetics; basic thiophenic compounds are not uniformly easy to remove. Hydrogen sulfide must be separated and managed downstream, creating an integrated treating and sulfur-recovery task.

The practical starting point is not the equation by itself; it is a description of the process boundary. Write down the feed, product, recovered co-product, waste and possible accumulation terms before choosing an indicator. In this lesson, the title hydrodesulfurization and sulfur balances indicates a specific decision lens within treating. Its purpose is to connect a measured or assumed characteristic to an operational consequence with a statement of what would prove the link. Separate primary evidence, indirect proxy and calculated prediction; those three categories are not interchangeable.

Quantity definition and model reasoning

A pseudo-first-order teaching relationship is C_S,out = C_S,in exp(−kτ). It is meaningful only if the measured sulfur basis and effective k remain consistent over the model conditions. Sulfur conversion is not the same as product mass yield.

To audit a model, begin with units. Write every mass flow on a common basis such as kg/h, every species flow on a consistent molar basis, and every energy rate as kW or MJ/h. Converting a component fraction from molar to mass basis requires component molar masses. A dimensionless index can be compared across scenarios only if the reference conditions and denominator have not changed. When the result is a percentage, specify percentage of what: total feed mass, converted feed, recovered product, sulfur atoms, or an observed time interval. The wrong denominator can change the interpretation even when the arithmetic is correct.

One useful way to check the numerical relationship is to inspect its limiting behavior. At zero conversion or removal, no product can appear or disappear through that mechanism; at complete removal, the tracked contaminant should be accounted for in another stream; at a cutpoint moved above or below the stated assay range, extrapolation should be rejected. A monotonic curve may be mathematically well behaved yet physically incomplete if secondary reactions, equilibrium or equipment capacity create a turning point outside the model.

Worked investigation: hypothetical data

A feed contains 12,000 ppmw sulfur; a toy model gives kτ = 3.5. Calculate the residual and removal fraction. State what additional data prove a real unit can reach that sulfur level.

Worked answer and interpretation

At kτ=3.5, the model outlet sulfur is 12,000 exp(−3.5)≈362.4 ppmw. The removal fraction is 1−exp(−3.5)≈0.9698, or 96.98%. This case illustrates why a large percentage removal need not satisfy a more stringent product target; species and catalyst data would be required for plant prediction.

Make a compact worksheet with four rows: starting assumptions, symbolic equation, numerical substitution and mass/energy/quality consistency check. Always place the units in the substitution row. An answer with four significant figures may be mathematically reproducible but should not be presented as a plant forecast when the assumed feed is only known roughly. Repeat the worksheet with one changed input, quantify the absolute and percentage change in the output, and ask which upstream measurement would be needed to defend that perturbation.

For numerical work, explicitly mark the inputs in this lesson as synthetic instructional values, including any yield factor, catalyst rate, representative boiling temperature or solvent efficiency. They must not be used as operating limits. If you wish to compare with published performance, define an independent, dated and source-specific dataset and evaluate whether its units and stream definitions match those of the exercise.

Plant integration and operating evidence

Report sulfur as ppmw or mass percent, identify assay and outlet method, measure catalyst activity and hydrogen availability, and reconcile sulfur to products and acid-gas systems. Specific product regulations require current method and jurisdiction verification.

Place the result in a real flowsheet context. Treating routes move contaminants from one stream to another. Removal percentage alone is incomplete until residual stream composition, solvent or catalyst management and disposal or recovery paths are defined. For hydrogen sulfide in particular, an absorber can shift hazard inventory without eliminating it. Ask where the upstream stream comes from, which downstream unit receives it, and how a change would shift at least one other required resource. A product-quality improvement might raise utility demand; more extensive contaminant removal might increase solvent regeneration; higher conversion may move load to gas compression or wastewater treatment. Draw the additional arrows on the block flow diagram, and do not claim that the local calculation has optimized the full plant.

For an evidence-based evaluation, design a measurement table with columns for variable, unit, sample or instrument location, time basis, quality method, expected repeatability and action on discrepancy. Include one direct process variable, one product property and one integration variable. A useful model-validation report should show residuals or errors for several cases, not only agreement at the nominal case used to choose parameters. Record potential confounding changes in feed, temperature, operator procedure and catalyst condition.

Assumption challenge and failure diagnosis

High fractional removal may still leave noncompliant residual sulfur if the inlet is elevated. Pseudo-first-order rates fail outside their calibration envelope.

A critical reader should ask which implicit assumptions were required to write the equation: steady state or dynamic accumulation, ideal mixing or plug flow, fixed or changing properties, homogeneous or multiphase composition, constant versus variable selectivity, and independence versus coupling of disturbances. Pick the assumptions that matter here. If a model fails, identify whether the defect originates in incorrect units, an invalid physical assumption, unmeasured variability, or numerical implementation. Merely adding complexity does not fix poor calibration data.

In practical terms, define a stop condition for inference: a measurement outside the sampled composition range, evidence of a new phase, an unexpected hazard, a negative concentration, or an implausible material imbalance invalidates the shortcut. Escalate to a validated model or an expert review before making a design or safety claim. The appropriate corrective action can be to collect data, not to alter equipment.

The engineering problem

A process diagram must include contactor, flash, heat exchange, regenerator and acid-gas disposal or sulfur-recovery route.

Unit 04 · Lesson 03 — Gas dehydration and water dew-point control

[Unit map](../units/unit-04.html) · [Open numerical laboratory](../labs/lab-12.html) · [Assessment](../assessments/unit-04.html) · [Applied project](../projects/unit-04.html)

The engineering problem

Gas dehydration protects cold processing, pipelines and downstream equipment from water condensation and hydrate risks. Glycol absorption and solid desiccants have distinct regeneration, contamination and turndown behavior. Reporting only water removal percentage can be misleading: downstream dew point depends on pressure, temperature and composition.

The practical starting point is not the equation by itself; it is a description of the process boundary. Write down the feed, product, recovered co-product, waste and possible accumulation terms before choosing an indicator. In this lesson, the title gas dehydration and water dew-point control indicates a specific decision lens within treating. Its purpose is to connect a measured or assumed characteristic to an operational consequence with a statement of what would prove the link. §4.5 Engineering Topic 4.5: Unit 04 · Lesson 04 — Sulfur recovery and tail-gas treatment

Unit 04 · Lesson 04 — Sulfur recovery and tail-gas treatment

[Unit map](../units/unit-04.html) · [Open numerical laboratory](../labs/lab-12.html) · [Assessment](../assessments/unit-04.html) · [Applied project](../projects/unit-04.html)

The engineering problem

Claus sulfur recovery reacts H₂S in an oxidizing and catalytic sequence to form elemental sulfur. The process includes combustion, heat recovery, condensation and catalytic conversion, often followed by tail-gas treatment. Sulfur recovery is fundamentally an element balance, and emission accounting must include the unrecovered sulfur species.

The practical starting point is not the equation by itself; it is a description of the process boundary. Write down the feed, product, recovered co-product, waste and possible accumulation terms before choosing an indicator. In this lesson, the title sulfur recovery and tail-gas treatment indicates a specific decision lens within treating. Its purpose is to connect a measured or assumed characteristic to an operational consequence with a statement of what would prove the link. Separate primary evidence, indirect proxy and calculated prediction; those three categories are not interchangeable.

A pedagogical multistage approximation gives residual fraction (1−η)^N for identical independent fractional-removal stages. Real Claus reactions approach equilibrium and stages are neither identical nor perfectly independent.

Worked investigation: hypothetical data

If 100 kmol/h of H₂S enters a conceptual recovery block with 95% sulfur atom recovery, estimate sulfur mass in t/h and sulfur remaining in other streams. Do not claim all unrecovered sulfur becomes SO₂.

Worked answer and interpretation

The feed carries 100 kmol H₂S/h, hence 100 kmol sulfur atoms per hour. At 95% conceptual recovery, 95 kmol S/h are recovered; using 32.065 kg/kmol gives about 3.046 t sulfur/h. Five kmol sulfur atoms per hour remain in other streams, equivalent to 0.160 t S/h. Their chemical forms are not specified.

Commissioning question

A fictional integrated refinery-and-petrochemicals teaching facility is reviewing its treating decisions after receiving evidence that the assumed nominal feed or operating condition is no longer reliable.
