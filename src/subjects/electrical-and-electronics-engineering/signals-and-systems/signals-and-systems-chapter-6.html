§6.1 Z transform and region of convergence

Unit 6, Lesson 1: Z transform and region of convergence

Unit theme: Z transforms and discrete-time filters Level: Undergraduate engineering (idealized models) Learning objective: The bilateral Z transform sums x[n]z^(−n); its ROC distinguishes sequence sidedness.

1. Concept and mathematical language

The bilateral Z transform sums x[n]z^(−n); its ROC distinguishes sequence sidedness. The role of this principle in z transforms and discrete-time filters is to connect the expression on paper with an observable prediction. Before manipulating symbols, define the independent variable, the range of allowed inputs, the output quantity, and the reference condition. In particular, identify whether the argument concerns a discrete-time signal. The same algebraic shape may describe completely different physical situations when time is measured in seconds in one case and by integer samples in another. A meaningful graph must identify its axes and units, the origin of the record, and whether the plotted values are exact or illustrative.
