§2.1 Impulse and step responses

Unit 2, Lesson 1: Impulse and step responses

Unit theme: Linear time-invariant systems in the time domain Level: Undergraduate engineering (idealized models) Learning objective: In LTI models, an impulse response h characterizes the zero-state input-output map; the step response integrates or sums the impulse response.

1. Concept and mathematical language

In LTI models, an impulse response h characterizes the zero-state input-output map; the step response integrates or sums the impulse response. The role of this principle in linear time-invariant systems in the time domain is to connect the expression on paper with an observable prediction. Before manipulating symbols, define the independent variable, the range of allowed inputs, the output quantity, and the reference condition.
