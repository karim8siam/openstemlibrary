Unit 8, Lesson 1: Cross-correlation and delay estimation

Unit theme: Measurement, correlation and integrated projects Level: Undergraduate engineering (idealized models) Learning objective: Correlation peaks indicate lag alignment of signals; normalization and sign convention determine the reported delay.

Correlation peaks indicate lag alignment of signals; normalization and sign convention determine the reported delay.
