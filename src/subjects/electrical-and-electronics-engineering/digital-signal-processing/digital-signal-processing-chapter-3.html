Specify ripple, rejection, transition width, group delay and coefficient precision for a 4 kHz digitized signal with a 1.2 kHz interfering tone. The unit provides the mathematical concepts: FIR filtering and group delay, Moving average and comb-shaped nulls, Windowed-sinc low-pass construction, Spectral inversion and high-pass FIR, FIR ripple, order and coefficient effects.   • Mathematical and physical specification: 20.

  • Boundary and violation tests: 20.
