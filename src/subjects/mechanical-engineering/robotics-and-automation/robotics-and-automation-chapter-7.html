Solved Problem Example 7.1: Calculation & Verification: PLC handshakes and interlocks

Problem Statement: An engineering practitioner analyzes a mechanical system governed by **PLC handshakes and interlocks** under steady operational loading.

Worked Engineering Derivation:

Stages 0.3, 1.0, 0.4 and 0.8 s total 2.5 s if none can overlap. Reproduce the substitution on paper, carry units through each stage and check the scale with an independent estimate. Precision in displayed decimal places does not demonstrate precise knowledge of link lengths, accelerations, friction coefficients or camera calibration. If inputs are rounded or synthetic, the outputs inherit those limitations; do not label them as measured robot performance. **Uncertainty audit.** Differentiate precision from accuracy: repeated values near one another may share a systematic offset. Use replicated trials and a calibration reference, and consider correlations when combining uncertainties. An error bound must be linked to a defined operating range.
