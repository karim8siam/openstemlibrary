Solved Problem Example 3.1: Calculation & Verification: Stress cycles and amplitude

Problem Statement: An engineering practitioner analyzes a mechanical system governed by **Stress cycles and amplitude** under steady operational loading.

Worked Engineering Derivation:

**Question:** Calculate alternating normal stress using max=130 MPa, min=-70 MPa. Using the explicit relationship for this simplified case, the arithmetic is `(130-(-70))/2`. The computed result is **100 MPa**. This is a synthetic, rounded result; it is not a measured or independently certified design capacity.
