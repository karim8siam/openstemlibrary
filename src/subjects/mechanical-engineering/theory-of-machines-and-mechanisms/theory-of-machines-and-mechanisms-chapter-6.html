Solved Problem Example 6.1: Calculation & Verification: Inertial torque of a rotor

Problem Statement: An engineering practitioner analyzes a mechanical system governed by **Inertial torque of a rotor** under steady operational loading.

Worked Engineering Derivation:

**Illustrative inputs:** Moment of inertia 0.12 kg·m²; angular acceleration 20 rad/s². **Calculation — Rotor accelerating torque:** **2.4 N·m**. These values were chosen for teaching, not measured from machinery. Put each input and its unit on a separate line in a reproducible calculation sheet, then verify the arithmetic with an independent computation. The sign of a rotational quantity indicates orientation; it does not imply an error. When the output is a force, check the implied work and reaction path. When it is a geometric dimension, check the assembly envelope and the full intended motion cycle, not only the illustrated pose. **Sanity test:** Vary one relevant parameter by 10% and classify the resulting trend as linear, inverse, quadratic or configuration-dependent.
