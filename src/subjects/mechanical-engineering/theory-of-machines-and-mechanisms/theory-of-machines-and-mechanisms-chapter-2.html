Solved Problem Example 2.1: Calculation & Verification: Rigid-body pose in two dimensions

Problem Statement: An engineering practitioner analyzes a mechanical system governed by **Rigid-body pose in two dimensions** under steady operational loading.

Worked Engineering Derivation:

**Illustrative inputs:** Body-fixed point radius 0.2 m, current body angle 60 degrees, origin at pivot. **Calculation — Rotated endpoint horizontal coordinate:** **0.1 m**. These values were chosen for teaching, not measured from machinery. Put each input and its unit on a separate line in a reproducible calculation sheet, then verify the arithmetic with an independent computation. The sign of a rotational quantity indicates orientation; it does not imply an error. When the output is a force, check the implied work and reaction path. When it is a geometric dimension, check the assembly envelope and the full intended motion cycle, not only the illustrated pose. **Sanity test:** Vary one relevant parameter by 10% and classify the resulting trend as linear, inverse, quadratic or configuration-dependent. Consider a near-limit case and ask which assumption would fail first. The calculation cannot independently prove its own assumptions: a derived value must eventually be compared with a separate observation, compatible published reference or physically grounded second model.
