   - Epistemology of Statistics: Positivism, empiricism, Hume's problem of induction, Popper's demarcation and falsificationism, Kuhn's paradigms, Lakatos' MSRP, Tukey's EDA vs CDA, Box's maxim, and underdetermination (Duhem-Quine).
   - Problem Formulation & Design: Operationalization, reflective vs formative constructs, Campbell-Stanley design taxonomy, threats to internal/external validity, Galton's regression to the mean derivation, Heckman selection models, and factorial designs with Fisher's hidden replication.
   - Measurement Theory & Psychometrics: Stevens' scale taxonomy, Classical Test Theory ($X = T + E$), parallel test reliability, Spearman-Brown prophecy, Cronbach's alpha derivation and $\tau$-equivalence, McDonald's $\omega_{\text{total}}$, Spearman's attenuation correction, MTMM matrices, and Item Response Theory (1PL, 2PL, 3PL) with DIF.

   - Modern Causal Inference: Potential outcomes (Neyman-Rubin model, SUTVA), Judea Pearl's causal DAGs, $d$-separation, Backdoor and Frontdoor identification theorems, Instrumental Variables (Wald estimator, 2SLS, LATE, Stock-Yogo weak instrument diagnostic), Regression Discontinuity Designs (Sharp/Fuzzy RDD), and Difference-in-Differences (parallel trends test, TWFE, Goodman-Bacon decomposition).
   - Research Ethics & Integrity: Belmont Report, IRB oversight, Sweeney's re-identification, $k$-anonymity, $l$-diversity, $\epsilon$-Differential Privacy via Laplace mechanism, QRPs ($p$-hacking, HARKing), publication bias diagnostics (inverted funnel plots, Egger's regression test, Rosenthal's fail-safe $N$, Duval-Tweedie trim-and-fill), and Registered Reports.
   - Scientific Communication & Reproducibility: Diagnostic causes of the replication crisis (Ioannidis PPV derivation), literate programming (Quarto, Jupyter, knitr), containerization (Docker), IMRaD architecture, Edward Tufte's graphical integrity (Lie Factor, data-ink ratio), ASA $p$-value statement, and international reporting guidelines (CONSORT, STROBE, PRISMA, SAMPL).
