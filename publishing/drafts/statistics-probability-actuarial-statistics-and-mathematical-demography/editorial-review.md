# Editorial and Mathematical QA Review: Actuarial Statistics and Mathematical Demography (Stat H-306, Stat H-312)

## Academic Rigor and Consistency Review
- All eight units reviewed for actuarial financial mathematics, life contingencies rigor, demographic renewal theory, and curriculum alignment with Dhaka University B.S. Honours Statistics curriculum (Stat H-306 & Stat H-312).
- Unit 1: Formalized force of interest, compound/nominal discounting, equation of value, Macaulay and modified duration, convexity, Redington and Shiu full immunization, Vasicek/CIR term structure models, and Nelson-Siegel/Svensson curves.
- Unit 2: Rigorously established annuities certain (immediate, due, continuous, arithmetically/geometrically varying), loan amortization schedules (prospective vs retrospective balance equivalence), sinking funds, Rule of 78, and Makeham bond formulas.
- Unit 3: Analyzed continuous survival models (future lifetime T_x, curtate lifetime K_x), Gompertz, Makeham, Weibull parametric survival laws, fractional age assumptions (UDD, CFM, Balducci), Lee-Carter stochastic mortality forecasting, and Whittaker-Henderson graduation.
- Unit 4: Proved multi-state Markov contingencies, Kolmogorov forward differential equations, multiple decrement theory, dependent vs independent rates, copula-based joint life modeling, and reversionary annuities.
- Unit 5: Formalized mathematical demography: Lotka's continuous renewal equation, intrinsic rate of natural increase, cumulant expansions, Leslie matrix spectral analysis, Fisher's reproductive value, multiregional demographic projection, and demographic ergodicity.
- Unit 6: Analyzed life insurance contingent claims, net single premiums (whole life, term, endowment), Equivalence Principle annual premiums, portfolio percentile premium principle, variance derivations (2A_x - A_x^2), and expense-loaded gross premiums.
- Unit 7: Formulated life annuity valuations, prospective and retrospective policy reserves, Thiele's differential equation, Hattendorff's theorem on reserve variance martingales, Modified Reserve Systems (FPT, Zillmerisation), and multi-state reserving.
- Unit 8: Modeled collective risk processes, compound Poisson/negative binomial aggregate claims, Panjer recursion algorithms, Cramér-Lundberg integro-differential ruin equations, Fast Fourier Transform (FFT) claims inversion, and pension costing methods (PUC vs EAN).
